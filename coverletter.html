Motivational letter

"If you set your goals ridiculously high and it's a failure,
you will fail above everyone else's success." - James Cameron

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am a Cloud Solutions Manager and Technical Lead for Business Applications at Dotcom Software, a Microsoft Solutions Partner, where I lead teams specialising in Microsoft Power Platform and Dynamics 365.

My technical leadership career is underpinned by a strong foundation in UX/UI and Product Design. I began my career designing user-centred digital products across the financial services, agricultural, pharmaceutical and retail sectors. Over time, my experience evolved beyond product design into broader technical and delivery leadership, enabling me to bridge design thinking, business requirements and technology to deliver scalable enterprise solutions.

In my current role, I lead the end-to-end technical delivery of Power Platform and Dynamics 365 solutions across multiple enterprise clients. I work closely with business stakeholders to translate complex requirements into practical and scalable solutions, while overseeing technical teams, delivery, governance, support and the continuous optimisation of business-critical applications.

Before transitioning into technical leadership, I built a strong career in Product Design. At BCX/Dotcom Software, I contributed to the design of a scalable point-of-sale solution for the pharmaceutical and retail sectors. At Standard Bank, I played a key role in developing OneFarm Grow, an innovative platform designed to digitise agricultural value chains and expand access to financial services for smallholder farmers and agribusinesses across Africa. My experience also includes working at Nedbank, where I designed user journeys for the Forex team and contributed to the High-Value Payments platform.

User research has been central to my career. My experience includes international travel to conduct on-the-ground research and interviews with smallholder farmers, as well as collaborating with stakeholders and multidisciplinary teams across multiple countries. This has given me a strong understanding of how technology, human behaviour and business requirements intersect when designing and delivering digital solutions.

Beyond the workplace, I have extended my leadership into multilateral and social-impact initiatives. I have contributed to the G20 Y20 (Youth20) official engagement group policy discussions and proposals during South Africa's G20 presidency, with a focus on education, artificial intelligence and innovation, while engaging with private-sector stakeholders including B20 (Business20) and Start-Up 20. I have also served as a South African delegate in initiatives involving UNFPA (United Nations Population Fund) and AUDA-NEPAD (African Union Development Agency and New Partnership for Africa's Development), contributing to public-private collaboration around youth employment, digital upskilling, entrepreneurship and the use of technology to harness Africa's demographic dividend potential.

My achievements include being nominated for Dotcom Software Solutions' Inspirational Leader Award and leading a four-person team to third place in the Standard Bank Group Hackathon. Together, my professional and external work reflects a career spanning technology, design, leadership and social impact, with a particular emphasis on using digital innovation to improve organisational outcomes and create broader societal value.

I hold a BA in Social Dynamics from Stellenbosch University, with majors in Psychology and Politics and a minor in Philosophy; an Advanced Diploma in User-Centred Design from the Red & Yellow Creative School of Business; and a Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science from the University of Essex Online. This multidisciplinary background enables me to combine social insight, design strategy and technical leadership to deliver impactful, user-centred and scalable digital solutions.

King Regards,

Victoria Thompson
